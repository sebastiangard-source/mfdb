How the nine brand lists overlap

The Cyprus Set (v0.49 export, October 18, 2026, with its US store counts and segments), CAKE, Croissant, Net-a-Porter, Nordstrom's designer list, Bloomingdale's, Saks, Neiman Marcus, and the retail tenants of 18 WS Development centers (read from each center's own directory, 22 October 2026; restaurants, services and offices left out). The page shows apparel and accessories brands only. Names are matched across lists (case, accents, and "&" ignored; known variants merged by hand). Bloomingdale's list is limited to apparel and accessories brands; its toys, baby, home, beauty and other brands were taken out. The Saks list is partial: it holds the Saks brands that match names in the other lists, plus about 170 confirmed through Neiman, and leaves out roughly 280 Saks-only designers that I couldn't separate reliably.
