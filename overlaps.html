How the eight brand lists overlap

The Cyprus Set (v0.49 export, October 18, 2026, with its US store counts and segments), CAKE, Croissant, Net-a-Porter, Nordstrom's designer list, Bloomingdale's, Saks, and Neiman Marcus. Names are matched across lists (case, accents, and "&" ignored; known variants merged by hand). Bloomingdale's list is limited to apparel and accessories brands; its toys, baby, home, beauty and other brands were taken out. The Saks list is partial: it holds the Saks brands that match names in the other lists, plus about 170 confirmed through Neiman, and leaves out roughly 280 Saks-only designers that I couldn't separate reliably.

Which brands to include

The page starts with apparel and accessories only. Bloomingdale's other categories have been removed, so its brands appear only here. Categories are my own classification from the brand names, so some are wrong; "Unclear" holds the ones I wasn't sure about.

Every brand

Dots show which lists carry the brand. Cyprus Set shows its tier; Net-a-Porter shows a star when the brand is featured there.
