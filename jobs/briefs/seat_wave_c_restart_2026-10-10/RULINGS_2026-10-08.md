## Three rulings, on your three questions

**1. Bands.** You were right that the brief gave ranges and no rule. Hold every band guess; do not
re-read anything. The Spectrum thread will set bands from the prices on file with one rule: by where
the **dress shirt and sweater** sit — under $150 Premium, $150–400 Accessible Luxury, above True
Luxury — with Sebastian overriding named cases. If a brand sells neither, the trousers and outerwear
decide. You do not need to carry `band_guess` in the full records; the price cells are what matter.

**2. Licensee- or operator-run stores count as the brand's own doors**, with an operator flag. The
map's reader is a customer, and the customer sees a Bass store. In `own_doors.csv` put the operator in
`note` ("operated by Authentic Brands licensee X", "franchise, Salt Water SA") and keep `format` =
own-door. Department-store counters remain concessions, as before. Outlets remain outlets.

**3. Marks.** The map uses the house's own mark as the storefront and logotype write it, dropping
corporate tails: G.H.BASS, Thursday Boot (not "Company"), Greyson (not "Clothiers"). Schott: use the
current logotype on schottnyc.com — read it and say which. Put the mark you read in `mark_as_written`
and keep the worklist key in `brand` so reconcile passes; the Spectrum thread renames keys on merge.
