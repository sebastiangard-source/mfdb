## Task

Read `rules_dials.md`. For each brand fill `dials_template.csv`, one row per dial (38 dials), returned as `dials.csv`. Each row has a proposed score, evidence (a quotation or a counted fact), the source URL and a confidence. Write `NC` where the pages give too little to judge.

Read at least ten men's product pages per brand, the About page and the lookbook. Count; do not summarise.
