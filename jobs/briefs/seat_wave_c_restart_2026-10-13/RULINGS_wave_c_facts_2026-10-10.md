## Rulings

**Stores under another name.** A door counts as the brand's own only if the brand's name is on the
door. So: Flint and Tinder's three Huckberry stores are Huckberry's, `0` own doors, and Huckberry goes
in `stockists.csv` as `other brand own door`; Needles' two Nepenthes stores likewise (`0`, Nepenthes NY
as the stockist); Castaway at Murray's Toggery is a stockist row, `0`. **Pilgrim Surf + Supply and
Undefeated** are different: the shop is the brand's own and carries its own label alongside others —
own door, `note` = "multi-brand shop under the brand's name". Same rule as C'H'C'M' and Lady White.

**Unusual door types.** Denim Tears' Atlanta residency and any dated installation: `pop-up`, with
dates. Brain Dead's Long Beach door: own-door if the brand's name is on it. Zadig & Voltaire's two
NYC "Archive" stores: `outlet` (an archive store sells past seasons at a markdown). Oxxford's Chicago
factory showroom: own-door, `note` = "factory showroom, by appointment" — it is the only place the
brand sells, and the map carries it as one door. Ledbury's Broad Street shop: own-door.

**Nautica**: file the "Comp. Value" figure as the regular price and the selling price in `note`,
and say in `NOTE.md` that the house prices against a reference, as Everlane and Joe's do; the full
read will treat it as a markdown house under `rules_prices.md`.

**Bands.** Hold every band guess, as ruled for wave B: the Spectrum thread sets bands from the full
read by where the dress shirt and sweater sit. Anglo-Italian, Lardini and Private White pricing like
True Luxury is a finding, not a problem; the facts-first band is a guess and the rule will decide.

**Marks.** As the logotype writes them, corporate tails dropped: Zadig&Voltaire (no spaces, as the
house writes it), Études (not "Études Studio" — the "Studio" is the company), Alan Flusser (not
"Custom"), Budd (not "Budd Shirtmakers"), Closed (the ® is a mark, not a name). The Spectrum thread
renames keys on merge; keep the worklist key in `brand`.

**Non-USD houses** (Beams, Hender Scheme, Undercover, Nigel Cabourn, Pini Parma): correct not to
convert. The full record carries native prices with the currency, as the map already does for eleven
houses. `np` for Hickey Freeman and Oxxford is right.
