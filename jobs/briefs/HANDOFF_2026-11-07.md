## Rules Sebastian set this segment

- **Everything settled by the thread:** every dial ends as evidence, a score from the garments (basis in the evidence, confidence low), or NA for sail only. Where a dial has a floor of 1, "none found" scores 1. Threads finish before seating; no unscored dials.
- **Named fibre with no percentage counts as 100% of that fibre** (noted in the style row).
- **Yen at 150 JPY/USD**, set by hand in `prices_override`; the seating script does not convert currency.
- **Shoes:** only 0, 3, 4, 5 are valid (1 and 2 are retired bands). Seat 0 for brands that don't make shoes (Iron Heart, UES, Denime).
- **Band rule:** mean of the dress-shirt low and sweater low; Basic needs tee at or under $35 and shirt under $80; Premium under $150; Accessible $150 to $400; True Luxury over $400; shoe houses use the shoe entry. Drop a slot with `prices_override={'T-shirt': None}` when the cheapest item isn't the core garment.
- **No "none" value below 1** on western, rock, workwear, surf or outdoor (the lint rejects 0). Sebastian asked for 0 on "none found"; that needs a schema and UI change and was offered but not decided.
- Deliverables to Sebastian are Word files when they are documents; briefs go as zip with `BRIEF.md`, `THREAD.md` and the templates, which is what every thread has followed. He prefers short, decisive replies he can paste into a thread.

## Seating mechanics that bit this segment

- `tools/seat_from_return.py <returns dir> <authored.py> <label>` refuses a brand that already exists. To reseat, delete `data/brands/<slug>.json`, rerun, then restore the old `region`, `doors` and `seat` by hand (save them first).
- The script appends a place record for every US own door, **including duplicates of shops already on the map**. After any seating run, diff `data/places.json` against HEAD and remove duplicates, then fix `region.o` and the index shifts.
- A brand's place links live in its `region` (`o` own stores, `s` stockists; both keys required). Removing a place shifts every later index, so reindex all brands' `region` lists (done for Britches and Englund's).
- Seats must stay dense 1..N; removing a brand means renumbering the ones above it.
- NYC places need `h` (neighbourhood). Sub-regions in `data/regions.json` must contain the city.
- Matching shops from a brand's locator by street number and city fails when the locator gives only a name (street `NC`); match on name and city too, or you add a duplicate (Plain Goods, Archery Close and Murray's were duplicates caught by the slug-uniqueness check).
- Each release needs a changelog entry; a corrected note needs its own new version.
- Brief defects in the shared template are fixed in the newer packs: `return_template.csv` header-only, no `mark_as_written` column (put marks in `note`).
