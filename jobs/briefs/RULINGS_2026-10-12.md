## Part 2 — for Sebastian

1. **Hart Schaffner Marx** — a licence-only Authentic Brands name: no catalogue, every price `np`, no
   doors, no styles, sold through Dillard's, Bloomingdale's and Nordstrom. The rule gives it "Premium by
   default, flagged", which would be wrong (its suits sit at $700–900). **Held.** Options: (a) seat at
   Accessible by hand with a note, (b) drop from the universe as unpriceable, (c) seat only when a retailer
   price read is allowed for licence-only brands.
2. **Kapital** — every price `NC`: the JPY site did not give a US-readable price in the time box, and
   there is no US store. **Held.** Options: (a) a stockist read (Kapital is at Nepenthes New York,
   Canoe Club, Blue in Green) allowed as the price source for a house with no US store, (b) hold until
   a thread re-reads kapital.jp in Chrome, (c) seat with eight `?` slots and no band.
3. **Rapha's band**. With no shirt or sweater the rule reads it as a shoe house on its $260 cycling
   shoe → Accessible. Its clothing entries are tee $70, trousers $175, jacket $190, which on the
   non-shoe cuts would be Premium. Is a cycling house a shoe house? Suggest: **no** — add a rule that the
   shoe-house fallback applies only where `shoes` is the house's core category, else the mean of
   whatever clothing staples are priced. The same question touches On (shoes $140 → Premium either
   way) and Hoka (shoes $60 → Premium either way), so Rapha is the only brand it moves.
4. **Salomon and Helly Hansen in Basic.** Both land there by the letter of the rule (tee $35 / $30,
   shirt none / $75). Helly Hansen's $75 shirt is a real dress-shirt entry; Salomon's is a tee beside
   $150 shells. If Basic is meant for mass basics and sportswear giants, Salomon fits; if it is meant
   for clothing houses, it does not. Suggest leaving both; say if not.
5. **Guam.** Vivienne Westwood's Tumon store was counted by the thread on the Puerto Rico precedent.
   The map covers the 50 states, DC and PR; Guam has no sub-region and the index cannot tag it, so it is
   held off the map with n = 2 and a note. Rule: PR only, or all territories (then the page needs GU, VI).
6. **Frye's licensee-made range** — counted as own label (Part 1, item 1). Confirm, or rule that a
   licensee-made category is `NONE` for prices and out of the style table.
7. **SurfStretch® (Birdwell)** — still open from 11 Oct: nylon like SurfNyl, or undisclosed. 11 styles.
8. **Register questions** (not the map): John Lobb's 13 US stockists include five Saks doors (chain
   rule); Rapha's Mike's Bikes and Landry's are bike-shop chains (door-one test for a non-clothing shop).
9. **Craft**: wave C batch 3 proposed Edward Green 4, Danner 4, Carmina 4 (a claimed but unshown
   factory), Budd 4; wave B batch 3 proposed John Lobb 4, Lucchese 4, Schott 4, Turnbull & Asser 4.
   Crockett & Jones 5 and Church's trueLux-vs-Alden are still open from 11 Oct.
10. **Blundstone craft** was not proposed (footwear-only, not read past heritage). Seated at 1 — a wrong
