## Ruled 13 October 2026 — the core-garment rule

The Basic test (tee ≤ $35 and shirt < $80) reads the brand's **core** tee and shirt: the plain, year-round
version of each, not the cheapest item that fits the word. A ribbed baby tee, a technical running tee, a
seasonal smocked or cropped top, a graphic or promotional tee is not the entry. Written into
`tools/seat_from_return.py`, `rules_prices.md` (wave C and restart), the women's price brief and
`data/bands.json`.

Applied to the 25 men's Basic brands on the evidence already on file: every entry tee and shirt is the
brand's core item (Gap Classic T-Shirt, Levi's Housemark, Carhartt K87, Uniqlo DRY, Vans Left Chest, Jos. A.
Bank Jersey Tee, Helly Hansen's 100% cotton Logo T-Shirt, Salomon's cotton Logo tee …) except Columbia's
and Timberland's graphic tees, whose plain tees are also under $35. **No men's band moves.** Helly Hansen
and Salomon stay Basic: their entries are their core items, and the earlier suggestion that this rule would
move them was wrong on inspection.

Aritzia (women's price pass, batch 2) is the case the rule was written for: its $28 ribbed baby tee and $58
smocked top are not its core tee and shirt (core tee $50 Essential, core shirts $88–148). Seats **Premium**
by hand at merge, with the core figures in the note.
