# Saks Fifth Avenue — Part A, links_brandset_2026-10-20

- **Method:** opened /designers (the Designers A-Z page; the guessed /c/ path went to the home page) once and read the /brand/<slug> links: 1,196 brands, men and women combined (Saks does not split them). Matched by folded name; URLs are the site's own brand pages. Counts from the "N Items" line on each brand page, 3 s per page. Time: ~40 min.
- **Matched:** 231 of 464 (227 exact, 4 alias: Faherty = Faherty Brand, Lafayette 148 = Lafayette 148 New York, Rene Ruiz = Rene Ruiz Collection, Valentino = Valentino Garavani). 233 NONE = not on the A-Z list. Names reconcile clean.
- **Wall:** after 170 good reads, a **slider "Is it you? ... verify you are not a robot"** page appeared. Per brief I did not interact with it, stopped, and left the remaining 61 matched brands `ND` (Saint Laurent onward in A-Z order, from Sachin Babi). Their URLs come from the A-Z list and were not opened. Chanel's page showed no count (ND for a different reason).
- **Not mapped:** Carhartt (only Carhartt WIP), Joe's (separate from Joe's Jeans), Elan, Maje, Roark (Fulton & Roark), Rowan (Carolyn Rowan Collection), Salomon (Yves Salomon), Soma (Nasomatto), Purple (Purple Brand exists; not confirmed as the worklist brand).
- **Doubtful:** Sea, Theory, CO, Camilla, Reiss categories not checked. Represent and Retrofête both read 77 items (possible coincidence, left as read). Brand pages span all Saks categories, men's and women's.
- **Brief note:** `url` has no legal empty value for NONE rows; I used a text placeholder.
