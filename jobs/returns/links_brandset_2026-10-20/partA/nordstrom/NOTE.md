# Nordstrom — Part A, links_brandset_2026-10-20

- **Method:** opened nordstrom.com/brands-list (All Brands; /brands and the /browse designer path gave 404 or a blank page) once and read the brand links (7,399: women, men and kids together). Matched by folded name. Brand pages are /brands/<name>--<id>; names, slugs and ids were read from the page, none guessed (slug = name with punctuation dropped, plus the id). No wall appeared on the 57 pages opened.
- **Matched:** 315 of 464 (311 exact, 4 alias: Blundstone = Blundstone Footwear, Lafayette 148 = Lafayette 148 New York, Pucci = Emilio Pucci, Carhartt WIP = Carhartt Work In Progress). 149 NONE = not in the list. Names reconcile clean.
- **Counts:** read "N items" on 57 brand pages (about 40 min box, then stopped). The other 258 matched brands are `ND`, either not reached or (for brands such as AllSaints, adidas, Burberry, Chanel, Chloé, Celine) the page is a brand-shop landing page with no item count. URLs for the unopened pages come from the list.
- **Two site entries for one name:** Off-White (Off-White, id 21134320, chosen; also "OFF WHITE", id 81032332) and SIMONMILLER (SIMONMILLER chosen; also "Simon Miller", id 10004200). Worth a look before use.
- **Not mapped:** Carhartt (only Work In Progress), Danielle Frankel (only a Birkenstock collab), Purple (Purple Brand not confirmed), Yves Salomon (site has Salomon), Rowan, Soma, Roller Rabbit; Pre-Owned Goyard/Omega/Rolex/TAG Heuer lines are not the brands.
- **Doubtful categories:** On, Theory, Elan, Camilla, CO, Reiss, Sea, Salomon not checked.
- **Brief note:** `url` has no legal empty value for NONE rows; I used a text placeholder. Women's-first does not apply: Nordstrom has one brand page per brand across genders.
