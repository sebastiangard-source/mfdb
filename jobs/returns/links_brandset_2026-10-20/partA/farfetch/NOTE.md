# Farfetch — Part A, links_brandset_2026-10-20

- **Method:** opened /designers/women once. The A–Z page loads one letter at a time, so I clicked through A to Z and 0-9 (what a person does) and read all 3,093 women's designers (5,382 links). Matched by folded name; URLs are Farfetch's own brand pages (`/shopping/women/<slug>/items.aspx`). Then opened each of the ~300 candidate pages once (about 2 s each) to confirm the brand actually has items. Time: ~75 min.
- **Matched:** 275 of 464 (269 exact, 6 alias: Alo = Alo Yoga, Diane von Furstenberg = DVF Diane von Furstenberg, G-Star = G-Star RAW, GUESS = GUESS USA, Malbon = Malbon Golf, Valentino = Valentino Garavani). 189 NONE. Names reconcile clean (464/464).
- **Wall:** none. No bot check, slider or error page appeared on the A–Z page or on any brand page.
- **Counts:** Farfetch shows no item total, only 96 products per page and "page 1 of N". So `listings` is a number only where the whole brand fits on one page (109 brands; counted the product cards). Otherwise `ND` ("more than one page (96 per page)").
- **NONE, three kinds:** (1) not on the A–Z list. (2) On the A–Z list but the page says "Sorry, no items" (24 brands, e.g. Bottega Veneta, Celine, Chanel, Dior, Hermès, Louis Vuitton, Goyard, J.Crew, Reiss, Splendid; several redirect to the sale listing). (3) The page has only Pre-Owned items, so not mapped as retail: Cartier, Rolex, Omega, Piaget, Harry Winston, Tiffany & Co., Van Cleef & Arpels (page URL in the note, in case Part B wants it).
- **Not mapped:** Carhartt (only Carhartt WIP), Purple (Purple Brand exists, not confirmed as the worklist brand), Saint Laurent (only Saint Laurent Eyewear on A–Z), Guizio (only Danielle Guizio, empty), RLX (RLX Ralph Lauren, empty).
- **Doubtful:** seven brands' links redirect to the sale listing (Belstaff, Boglioli, Ciao Lucia, Frescobol Carioca, Hill House Home, Isaia, Sunspel); count is sale items only, noted per row. Dries Van Noten and Fjällräven each have two pages; used the one with items. Jil Sander+ not folded into Jil Sander. Category not checked for Theory, Sea, CO, Camilla, On, Still Here. A small count (1–5) may be a brand's last items in stock, not a real range.
- **Scope:** women's side only; a brand sold only in the men's section would show NONE.
