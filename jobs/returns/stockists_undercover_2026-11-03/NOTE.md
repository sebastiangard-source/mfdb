## 3. What contradicted the existing record or the brief

1. **Wrong or hijacked domains.** The obvious domains are traps. dsmny.com is an unrelated parked page. maxfield.com gave a certificate error (not opened); the shop is maxfieldla.com. nakamuraya.com is a Japanese software company; the shop is nakamurayaportland.com. modernappealingclothing.com is now a gambling-spam site; the shop is macmodernappealingclothing.com. shopblake.com is a closed Shopify store; the shop is blakechicago.com. Blake and Ikram identities were confirmed by address and phone matching the locator.
2. **Schema gap: no women's-only value.** The `gender` enum has only mens_only, multigender and womens_with_mens_tail. Blake, Ikram and Relish are women's shops, so I wrote `womens_only`, which will fail validation until Sebastian rules. Relish added a small men's selection in 2022 (InsideHook) but its current site list is headed womens; Sebastian may prefer womens_with_mens_tail for Relish.
3. **Dover Street Market brand lists differ.** The brief expected them to be the same. New York's floor guide has 135 entries (122 distinct once women's/men's suffixes are dropped); Los Angeles's store guide has 122 (121 distinct), including Palace, Greg Ross and Goyagoma, and omitting Lemaire. LA's own address is 606–608 Imperial Street (locator: 608).
4. **Maxfield's city.** The shop's site calls the Melrose store West Hollywood; the locator says Los Angeles. ZIP 90069 is the locator's. Maxfield also has a Malibu door (3939 Cross Creek Road) not in the locator.
5. **Atelier New York has two 8th Street doors**: men's at 20 W 8th (the locator row) and women's at 29 W 8th (+1 212.473.3170, not in the locator). Commercial Observer (2 September 2026) reports Atelier signed for 181 MacDougal Street, opening in four to six months, and that Atelier declined to say whether it leaves either 8th Street site. The row-18 address may change.
6. **Alan Bilzerian's locator line is WOMENS only**, but the shop has a men's floor and men's brands. Its own About page says the team "started over 40 years ago"; Boston Magazine (26 June 2024) gives 1967 in Worcester.
7. **Relish changed hands.** The site says Lindsay Rieling now owns it; InsideHook (November 2022) named Nancy Pearlstein. The date of the handover is not stated.
8. **H.Lorenzo's phone.** The locator gives +1 213-387-2206; the shop's Locations page gives 310-652-7039 for the flagship that opened in 2026. The locator's number may be a closed store's.
9. **The brief's "Alan Bilzerian is the only one in the Northeast"** was overtaken by rows 15 and 18 (Philadelphia and New York), which Sebastian ruled to do right after it.
10. **Nordstrom shop names:** the brief says use the name the shop writes. The store pages say Nordstrom NYC Flagship and Nordstrom Downtown Portland, so those are used; the template named them Nordstrom New York and Nordstrom Portland. Row numbers are unchanged. Likewise row 10 reads Kith Manhattan.
11. **`schema.json` says the story register is that of "the seven examples"**; `shop_example.json` holds one. I used that one.
12. **Brand counts are as published, not deduplicated.** They include collaborations, sub-lines, own labels and duplicates, and for Maxfield and Joan Shepp the site's own scope. `ub` counts distinct canonical brands only. Collaboration-only entries (for example "Wales Bonner x Y-3" at Atelier) were not credited as the brand itself; sub-lines of Comme des Garçons, Issey Miyake, Yohji Yamamoto, Junya Watanabe and Maison Margiela were credited to the house name. `reconcile.py` was run on every list; the near misses it reports are the capital-letter rewrites, and the exact canonical names are in `matched_brands`.
