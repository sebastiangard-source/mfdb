# Taxonomy: The Cyprus Brand Set, segments (proposal, 15 October 2026)

Twenty-three segments for 420 brands. The first fifteen are the men's map's segments as ruled on 14 October, kept so the two read alike. The last eight are new, for women's markets the men's taxonomy has no room for. Examples are the largest by store count.
