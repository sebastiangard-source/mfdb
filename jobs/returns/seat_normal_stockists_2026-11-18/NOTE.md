## 2. Stockists
- **How I counted** (recounted from the locator's own data, `window.SCASLWtb.locations`): 946 rows = 11 own-brand + 935 retailer rows. Of the 935: **894 US rows** in **49 states** (none in HI or DC), **37 Canada** (the earlier "36" missed one with a blank province), **2 elsewhere** (Baker's Bay Golf Resort, Abaco; Cabo del Sol), and **2 junk "Dillards" rows**, one with a PO Box 8037 Little Rock AR address (state field AZ) and one all-zero row (city and zip "0"). The earlier "897 US" counted the junk rows and some others loosely; **894 is the better figure.** Many rows have a blank country, so US rows were assigned from the state field (state names mapped to codes) and, for 7 rows with no state, from city or zip (Peppermill NV, Roosterbus AZ, Blu Home FL, margos and co MI, Prairie Threads IL, Soul & Cedar CT, Town & Country GA; the zips on the last three look wrong, so those states are my judgment).
- **Names:** 731 distinct US retailer names after merging spellings that differ only by punctuation or apostrophe (Dillard's/Dillards; "Rambler", "Rambler (Myrtle Beach)", "Rambler Peoria" as one; "Bolero" with "Bolero Menswear"). I did not merge look-alikes I could not confirm ("Active Endeavors" with "Active Endeavors Of Des Moines"; "Rooster Bus" with "Roosterbus"; "Him Gentleman’s Boutique" with "HIM Men's Clothing Store"), so the distinct count is slightly high. Von Maur: not confirmed (no brand page found; press only). Target / Target Plus: not confirmed. Macy's, Belk, Bloomingdale's, Neiman Marcus, Saks: none in the locator, pages not checked.
