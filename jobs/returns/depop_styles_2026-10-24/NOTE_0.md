# Style tags: what we learned (Depop, Vinted, luxury sites)

Oct 24, 2026 · @Sebastian

## Bottom line

No site we checked shows aesthetic style tags (Y2K, Boho, Preppy) to a signed-out shopper, so the Depop style-mix brief cannot run as written. Depop, Vinted and ten premium, luxury or resale sites expose category and attribute filters only. Where a filter is labelled "Style" (Nordstrom, Shopbop), it lists garment cuts such as A-Line or Babydoll, not how shoppers see a brand.

## Depop pilot (Part 0)

None of the brief's three methods works signed out, so the batches were not started. The pilot read the women's pages of Free People, Lululemon, Gucci, Carhartt WIP and Talbots, plus one Free People listing, on 24 October 2026.

- **A, facet counts:** no. The filter bar offers Category, Brand, Price, Size, Color, Condition and On sale. The mobile Filter drawer lists the same ones. There is no Style filter and no counts.
- **B, filter counts:** not possible without a Style filter. I did not try URL parameters, since the brief limits the read to pages as a shopper sees them.
- **C, listing sample:** no. The listing page shows category, color, size, condition, brand and seller, but no style tags in the visible text or page source.
- **Style list:** not found on any page, so `depop_style_list.txt` was not produced.

Header counts read for the five pilot brands:

| --- | --- | --- |
| Free People | 687K | 683K |
| Lululemon | 1.11M | 1.1M |

Depop's style tags may only be visible signed in or in the app. Both are outside the brief, which forbids signing in.

## Vinted

Vinted has no style filter and does not give a usable whole-page count. A Free People search offers Category, Size, Brand, Condition, Color, Price and Material filters. The results header caps at "500+ results". The one listing I tried had been removed, so I could not confirm what a live listing shows; I saw no sign of a style field. I read it in a separate tab and left your open sell form alone.

## Premium, luxury and resale sites

None of the ten sites has aesthetic style tags. I read each site's women's dresses page signed out; where noted, I opened the full filter panel.

| Site | Filters and tags found | Style tags? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Net-a-Porter | Category, Trending (Exclusive, Runway, New Designer, Bestseller), Color, Fabric, Designer, Sleeves, Print, Size, Price. Occasion links (Party, Wedding Guest, Vacation) are not filters. | No |
| Farfetch | Category, Brand, Size, Dress Length, Silhouette, Fit, Neckline, Pattern, Sleeve, Material, Color, Price, Promotion. No counts. | No |
| Revolve | "By Style" list is garment types (Bodycon, Shift, Slip Dresses, Sundresses). Also Trending Now and What to Wear occasion lists. Total count only (21,553 items). | Garment types only |
| Nordstrom | Full drawer has a "Style" filter of cuts (A-Line, Babydoll, Ball Gown, Button-Up, Cutout), plus Occasion, Length, Material. No counts. | Garment cuts only |
| SSENSE | Sale, Category, Designer, Color. A security check appeared first and cleared on its own after about 15 seconds. | No |
| Mytheresa | Categories, Designer, Size, Material, Color, Sleeve, Pattern. | No |
| The RealReal | Category counts (Dresses 241,185), Designers, Condition, Color & Patterns, Editors' Picks. Menu has editorial "Occasion" and "Shop By Style" links, not filters. | No |
| Vestiaire Collective | Category, Designers, Condition, Sizes, Colors, Materials, Price, shipping and seller options. Quick links are occasions and lengths. | No |
| Grailed | Department, Category, Size, Color, Condition, Price, with counts per category. | No |
| Shopbop | Landed on the shoes page, where "Style" and "Toe Style" are shoe cuts. The dresses page was not reached. | Partly checked |

Revolve's "Cool Girl Gowns" and "Chic Day Dresses" trend lists are the nearest thing to a shopper-view label. They work at category level, not brand level.

## What it means and options

A brand-by-brand style mix for the 431 Explorer brands cannot come from any public filter we found. The batches are on hold, and nothing was written for them. The options:

- **Drop the style read** and use our own segments alone.
- **Build the mix from listing titles and descriptions** on Depop, which needs a text-reading method and a decision on sample size.
- **Allow a signed-in Depop read,** if style tags show there. The brief currently forbids signing in, so this needs your call.
- **Try other sources** such as Pinterest or Lyst, which I have not checked.
