# What the map is missing — 8 October 2026

206 brands seated: 61 Premium, 74 Accessible Luxury, 71 True Luxury. 29 queued since 13 September,
59 in the Boyds sift, three more briefed yesterday, 805 names in the trade register. This note asks a
different question from the registers: not *what have we seen* but *what would a knowledgeable reader
look for and not find*. The answer is 171 names in `candidates_2026-10-08.csv`, in three tiers, with a
sift brief for the first two.

**Tier A (36)** — a reader would notice the absence: big US own-store footprints or houses the map's own
registers are built around. **Tier B (54)** — fills a register the map is thin on, with a known US door.
**Tier C (81)** — probably no US doors; belongs in the trade register unless a ruling makes an exception.

## Where the holes are

**1. The footwear wave is the biggest hole, and it has two halves.** The map seats three shoe houses
(Tecovas, Rothy's, Golden Goose) and the price slot "Shoes" on every card. Missing are the trad shoe
canon that every register shop carries — Alden (Middleborough, MA), Allen Edmonds, Johnston & Murphy,
Cole Haan, G.H. Bass, Sperry — and the sneaker houses that half the collaborations on the map run
through: Nike, adidas, New Balance (Boston), Converse (Boston), Vans, Asics, Hoka, On, Salomon. Plus the
English makers with New York shops (Crockett & Jones, John Lobb, Church's, Edward Green) and Birkenstock
and Dr. Martens, which have more US stores than most of the Premium band. Twenty-nine names, most of
them Tier A.

**2. The mass floor has never been ruled.** Levi's, Carhartt, Lacoste and Banana Republic are seated;
Tommy Hilfiger is on the bench as "mass-market"; Uniqlo, Abercrombie & Fitch (200-plus stores, a
revived men's range), Calvin Klein (queued), UNTUCKit (80-plus stores), Gap, Zara, Mango, Muji, Jos. A.
Bank and Lands' End are nowhere. The Premium band's stated range is $80–300 for basics; a Uniqlo tee is
$15, an Abercrombie tee $30, a Levi's tee $25. The map needs one sentence saying where the floor is and
why, and then the tier-A mass names either seat or go to the bench with that sentence as the reason.

**3. The sportswear giants.** Nike and adidas are the most-collaborated-with brands on earth and the
collabs thread will return them as partners of a dozen seated houses. New Balance is a Boston company
with a Todd Snyder and an Aimé Leon Dore line. None is seated. They are also the Premium band's largest
own-store footprints by a wide margin. Whether the map wants them is a ruling, but it is a ruling that
should be made rather than left.

**4. The street register has no anchor.** Kith, ALD, BAPE, Madhappy, Palm Angels and Rhude are seated;
Supreme (own stores in NYC, LA, SF, Chicago), Stüssy (NYC, LA, SF, Honolulu), Off-White, Palace, Human
Made and Carhartt WIP are not. A street dial with no Supreme on it is a dial a reader will not trust.
Carhartt WIP raises a rule: it is a license (Work In Progress, Basel), not Carhartt, with its own US
stores; the collabs brief excludes licenses from collaborations, but nothing says whether a licensed
house can seat under its own name.

**5. The Northeast's own houses.** The map's region is the Northeast and it is missing L.L.Bean
(Freeport, 50-plus stores, a Todd Snyder partner), Orvis (Vermont, 60-plus), Alden, New Balance,
Converse, Tracksmith (Boston, Trackhouses in Boston, NYC and Wellesley), Ministry of Supply (Boston,
own stores), Paul Stuart (Madison Avenue since 1938), Rowing Blazers (NYC), Kiel James Patrick (Rhode
Island), Castaway (Nantucket), Rancourt and Quoddy (Maine shoes). Some are Tier C on doors, but a
Northeast map without L.L.Bean and Alden is odd.

**6. Luxury houses with US stores.** Louis Vuitton is the largest luxury house in the world and is not
on the map; its absence is the first thing a luxury reader will notice. Dunhill, Bally, Tod's, Chrome
Hearts, Issey Miyake (Homme Plissé), Balmain, Lanvin, Marni, Kenzo, Vivienne Westwood, Turnbull &
Asser all have US stores and none is seated. Emporio Armani and Moncler Grenoble raise the sub-line
rule again (RRL and Purple Label are their own keys; Y-3, Needles, Homme Deux are open).

**7. Outdoor and outerwear.** Arc'teryx, Patagonia, Canada Goose, Moncler and Herno are seated. Missing
with US stores: The North Face (queued), Columbia, Eddie Bauer, Fjällräven, Woolrich (the oldest US
outdoor brand, NYC flagship), Mackage and Moose Knuckles (the Canadian outerwear houses with NYC and
Boston stores), Snow Peak and Nanamica (NYC), Aether, Helly Hansen, Pendleton (queued), and the ski
houses the ski register has nobody for — Kjus, Bogner, Fusalp, Aztech, Perfect Moment.

**8. Golf has the DTC wave and not the courses' own brands.** Peter Millar, johnnie-O, TravisMathew,
Malbon, Rhoback, Criquet, Redvanly, J.Lindeberg, RLX seated. Missing: G/FORE (own stores), Greyson
(queued), Holderness & Bourne, Bad Birdie, Eastside Golf, Kjus. Most are Tier B or C on doors.

**9. The trad and prep canon.** J.Press, Brooks Brothers, Vineyard Vines, Southern Tide, J.McLaughlin,
Sid Mashburn, Drake's seated. Missing: Paul Stuart, Gant (the bench's own "one that matters"), Rowing
Blazers, Fred Perry (US stores), Ledbury, Onward Reserve, Hart Schaffner Marx (queued, six register
shops), Hickey Freeman, Alan Flusser, Oxxford. Paul Stuart and Gant are Tier A.

**10. The British and Japanese middles.** Both registers are thin in Accessible Luxury because the
houses that fill them have no US store: Private White V.C., Oliver Spencer, Universal Works, Nigel
Cabourn, Studio Nicholson, Anglo-Italian; Beams, Orslow, Undercover, Kolor. Tier C — trade register,
not the map, unless the bar bends. Kapital, Nanamica, Snow Peak and Human Made have NYC stores and are
the Japanese houses that can seat.

## Rulings this needs, in order

1. **The floor.** Where Premium starts and why. Decides Uniqlo, Abercrombie, Tommy Hilfiger, Calvin
   Klein, UNTUCKit, Gap, Zara, Mango, Jos. A. Bank, Nautica.
2. **Sportswear giants.** Nike, adidas, New Balance, Converse, Vans, Puma: in or out as a class.
3. **Shoe houses.** Does a house that sells only shoes seat, with seven `NONE` price slots? Tecovas and
   Rothy's say yes; if so, the footwear wave is 29 names.
4. **Licenses.** Carhartt WIP as its own key, or never.
5. **Sub-lines.** Emporio Armani, Moncler Grenoble, Y-3, Needles, Homme Deux: own key, fold, or never —
   the RRL precedent says own key when it has its own stores.
